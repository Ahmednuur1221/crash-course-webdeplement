### Labels

-   **Computer:** Sends and receives data.
-   **Router:** Directs data to the correct destination.
-   **ISP (Internet Service Provider):** Connects your home to the
    internet.
-   **Internet Routers:** Pass data across networks worldwide.
-   **Server:** Stores and delivers websites or online services.
